About us

Built for local parents

You want your child to actually learn — not just be handed to whoever's available first. That's the real problem parents keep running into: no way to know if a tutor is truly qualified, no way to tell if classes are even happening once the fees start, and no way to know it's working until the report card shows up.

  • Every tutor is personally checked — degree, subject knowledge, and experience — before they're ever listed.
  • You see attendance for every class, marked by the tutor the same day, so you always know teaching actually happened.
  • You get real progress updates from the tutor, not silence until the next exam.
  • Every tutor stays within 5km of your home, so travel time doesn't eat into your child's lesson time.
  • Other parents' reviews are visible on every profile, so you're never picking blind.

We built this because every parent deserves a way to find a tutor that feels safe, not just convenient.
